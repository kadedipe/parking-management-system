1. Getting Started
Q1.1: What is the Parking Management System?
A: The Parking Management System is a comprehensive platform that allows users to:

Find and book parking spots in real-time

Manage multiple vehicles

Use EV charging stations

Make secure payments

Track parking history and receipts

Earn loyalty points

Q1.2: How do I create an account?
A: Follow these steps:

Download the app from App Store/Google Play or visit the website

Tap "Sign Up"

Fill in your name, email, and password

Agree to the Terms of Service

Verify your email address

Complete your profile

Q1.3: Is the app available for both iOS and Android?
A: Yes! The app is available on:

iOS: Version 14.0+

Android: Version 10.0+

Web: All modern browsers

Q1.4: Do I need an account to use the app?
A: You need an account to book parking, manage vehicles, and make payments. However, you can browse available parking spots without logging in.

2. Account & Profile
Q2.1: How do I reset my password?
A:

Tap "Forgot Password?" on the login screen

Enter your registered email address

Check your email for a password reset link

Click the link and follow the instructions to set a new password

Q2.2: Can I change my email address?
A: Yes!

Go to Profile → Edit Profile

Tap on the email field

Enter your new email address

Click Save Changes

Verify your new email address

Q2.3: How do I delete my account?
A:

Go to Profile → Settings → Account

Tap Delete Account

Confirm your decision

Enter your password

Tap Confirm Delete

⚠️ Note: This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Q2.4: Can I have multiple profiles?
A: No, each account is linked to one user profile. However, you can manage multiple vehicles under one account.

Q2.5: How do I enable two-factor authentication?
A:

Go to Profile → Settings → Security

Tap Two-Factor Authentication

Scan the QR code with your authenticator app

Enter the verification code

Tap Enable

4. Finding & Booking Parking
Q4.1: How do I find available parking?
A:

Open the app and go to the Parking tab

Search for a location or use your current location

Filter results by:

Distance

Price

Availability

Amenities

View available spots on the map or list

Q4.2: How do I book a parking spot?
A:

Select a parking lot from the list or map

Choose an available spot

Select date and time

Choose your vehicle

Review the price summary

Tap Confirm Booking

Q4.3: Can I book a spot in advance?
A: Yes! You can book spots up to 30 days in advance.

Q4.4: How far in advance can I book?
A: You can book parking spots up to 30 days in advance.

Q4.5: What happens if the spot is not available when I arrive?
A: The app provides real-time availability. If a spot becomes unavailable, you will be notified and can choose another spot.

Q4.6: Can I extend my booking?
A: Yes!

Go to My Bookings

Select your active booking

Tap Extend

Choose additional hours

Confirm extension

Q4.7: How do I cancel a booking?
A:

Go to My Bookings

Select the booking you want to cancel

Tap Cancel

Select a reason (optional)

Confirm cancellation

Q4.8: Is there a cancellation fee?
A: Cancellation fees depend on how far in advance you cancel:

2+ hours before: Full refund

1-2 hours before: 50% refund

Less than 1 hour: No refund

5. Payments & Billing
Q5.1: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept:

💳 Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)

💰 PayPal

📱 Google Pay

🏦 Bank transfers (for monthly subscriptions)

Tap Save

Q5.4: How do I set a default payment method?
A:

Q5.5: How do I view my payment history?
A:

Go to Profile → Payment History

View all past transactions

Tap any transaction to see details

Q5.6: How do I get a receipt for my payment?

Payment History

Q5.7: What currency is used for payments?

6. EV Charging
Q6.1: How do I find EV charging stations?
A:

Go to the Charging tab

View nearby stations on the map

Filter by:

Connector type

Charging speed

Availability

Select a station for details

Q6.2: What connector types are supported?
A: Supported connector types include:

🔌 Type 1

🔌 Type 2

🔌 CCS

🔌 CHAdeMO

🔌 Tesla

Q6.3: How do I start a charging session?
A:

Select a charging station

Choose an available connector

Select your vehicle

Tap Start Charging

Connect your vehicle to the charger

Monitor charging progress

Q6.4: How much does charging cost?
A: Charging costs are based on:

Energy consumption (kWh)

Time (per minute)

Station pricing (varies by location)
You'll see the estimated cost before starting.

Q6.5: Can I reserve a charging station?
A: Yes! You can reserve a charging station up to 30 minutes in advance.

Q6.6: What happens if I stop charging early?
A: You will only be charged for the energy used and time spent. The session will end immediately.

Q6.7: How do I monitor my charging session?
A: During charging, you can see:

🔋 Battery percentage

⚡ Charging speed

⏱️ Time elapsed

💰 Cost accrued

⏳ Estimated completion time

Q7.7: Can I book for someone else?

Q7.8: How do I view my booking history?

9. Technical Issues
Q9.1: The app is crashing. What should I do?
A:

Restart the app

Clear the app cache

Update the app to the latest version

Restart your device

If the issue persists, contact support

Q9.2: The map is not loading. What should I do?
A:

Check your internet connection

Enable location services

Restart the app

Try using the list view instead of map view

If the issue persists, contact support

Q9.3: My location is not being detected correctly.
A:

Enable GPS/location services

Ensure app has location permissions

Move to an open area for better GPS signal

Toggle location services off/on

If the issue persists, contact support

Q9.4: The app is slow/lagging.
A:

Clear app cache

Close other apps running in the background

Check your internet connection

Update app to the latest version

If the issue persists, contact support

Q9.5: How do I update the app?
A:

iOS: App Store → Updates → Parking App

Android: Play Store → My Apps & Games → Parking App

Web: Refresh the browser or clear cache

Q9.6: The app is not working offline.
A: The app requires an internet connection for most features. However, you can:

View your bookings offline (limited)

Access saved payment methods

View cached parking data
