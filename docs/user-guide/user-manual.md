1. Introduction
1.1 Welcome
Welcome to the Parking Management System! This comprehensive platform allows you to:

🔍 Find available parking spots in real-time

📅 Book and reserve parking spots in advance

⚡ Locate and use EV charging stations

💳 Make secure payments

🚗 Manage your vehicles

📱 Access features on mobile and web

1.2 System Requirements
Platform	Requirements
Mobile App	iOS 14+ / Android 10+
Web App	Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+
Internet	Stable internet connection
Location	GPS enabled for location services
2. Getting Started
2.1 Account Creation
Step 1: Download the App
text
📱 iOS: App Store -> Search "Parking App"
📱 Android: Play Store -> Search "Parking App"
🌐 Web: https://parkingapp.com
Step 2: Create Account
Open the app and tap "Sign Up"

Fill in your details:

Full Name: Your complete name

Email Address: Valid email for verification

Phone Number: Contact number

Password: At least 8 characters with letters and numbers

Agree to the Terms of Service

Tap "Create Account"

Step 3: Email Verification
Check your email for verification link

Click the link to verify your account

Return to the app to continue

11.3 FAQs
Question	Answer
How do I book a parking spot?	Search for parking -> Select spot -> Choose date/time -> Confirm booking
How do I cancel a booking?	Go to My Bookings -> Select booking -> Tap Cancel
